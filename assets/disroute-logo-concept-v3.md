Created with the built-in image generation tool. Concept preview only; not installed as the application icon.

## Design prompt

Design a sophisticated original app symbol for DisRoute, a selective network routing desktop app. Create a substantial custom uppercase D monogram with a purposeful rising diagonal negative-space cut suggesting a route finding its way through. Use precise optical balance, a bold memorable silhouette, a generous interior counter, softly rounded exterior corners and crisp cut terminals. Use a pale mint #92DBB5 symbol on dark ink #0B0E13, with generous breathing room. Moderate complexity and flat vector-style geometry. Avoid shields, circuitry, nodes, padlocks, globe imagery, wordmarks, mockups, gradients, texture and shadows.
