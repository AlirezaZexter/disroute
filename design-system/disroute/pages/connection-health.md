# Connection health panel

Follow the shipped desktop interface and its locally bundled Vazirmatn, superseding the older master file's Inter and marketing-page guidance for this panel.

- Canvas #0b0e13; inset #0d1118; text #edf1f7; secondary #96a1b2; measured-success mint #92dbb5; failure #f07178.
- Use 13px supporting labels and 15px headings, with 23px tabular readings. Persian labels are RTL; chronological measurements run oldest to newest left-to-right with explicit labels.
- One instrument panel below the connection controls: heading and manual check, then measured history beside three readings, then one contextual explanation. Collapse to stacked rows below 820px.
- Every colored bar is a real HTTPS sample, failed samples include a cross, and empty samples remain neutral. No decorative waveform or made-up quality percentage.
- The status heading is the sole live announcement in this panel; seconds and individual bars do not announce continuously. Respect existing reduced-motion preferences and keyboard focus styles.
- Review: preserve the current brand and ordinary-language controls. Avoid adding a second connect button or repeating the routing diagram.
